  1. Place the phone flat and line it up against a straight edge (table edge, box, ruler), with the top of the phone pointing the way you want to call +Y.
  2. Press Start and keep it completely still until the button turns red.
  3. Move smoothly, ideally in under 5 s. For a long or awkward route, stop part-way (e.g. on the door sill) and hold the phone completely still until the green ✓ Checkpoint tick appears (about a second): each checkpoint resets the drift.
  4. Stop just above the destination, then set it down gently. Don't let it knock onto the surface.
  5. Leave it still for ~2 s, then press Stop.
